Prompt 1 — Pittsburgh vs Michigan representation
Expected output filename: illustration_pittsburgh_vs_michigan.png

Create a clean academic vector-style illustration for a Bachelor's Thesis in Artificial Intelligence. The diagram must explain the conceptual difference between Pittsburgh-style and Michigan-style evolutionary representations for a single-hidden-layer neural network.

The image should be horizontal, suitable for an A4 thesis page, with a white or very light background, readable labels, simple arrows, and a modern academic style. Do not use 3D effects, cartoons, decorative gradients, or excessive text.

Divide the diagram into two clearly separated panels:

Left panel title: "Pittsburgh representation"
Show a small evolutionary population where each individual is a complete neural network or complete hidden layer. Each individual should look like a compact one-hidden-layer MLP: input nodes, a full hidden layer, and an output node. Show several full-network individuals inside a population box. Add a short label: "One individual = one complete model". Add an arrow from the population to a box labelled "Evaluate full models".

Right panel title: "Michigan representation"
Show a population where each individual is a single hidden neuron, not a full network. Draw many individual neuron icons inside a population box. Add a short label: "One individual = one hidden neuron". Show selected neurons being assembled into one hidden layer, then connected to an output layer. Add a box labelled "Population forms hidden layer".

The visual message must be clear: Pittsburgh evolves whole models; Michigan evolves individual neurons that cooperate to form the hidden layer.

Use only short English labels inside the image:
- "Pittsburgh representation"
- "Michigan representation"
- "One individual = one complete model"
- "One individual = one hidden neuron"
- "Evaluate full models"
- "Select cooperative neurons"
- "Selected hidden layer"

Important constraints:
- Do not draw Michigan individuals as complete networks.
- Do not draw Pittsburgh as individual neurons.
- Do not show backpropagation or gradient arrows.
- Do not include datasets, accuracies, or experimental results.
- Do not put long explanatory paragraphs inside the image.

Suggested caption for the thesis: "Conceptual difference between Pittsburgh and Michigan evolutionary representations. Pittsburgh individuals encode complete models, while the proposed Michigan formulation represents each hidden neuron as an individual and assembles the population into a hidden layer."

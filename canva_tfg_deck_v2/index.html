Conclusions

Conclusions and Future Work

Limitations

  • Single-hidden-layer architecture
  • Limited benchmark scope
  • Specific Pittsburgh baselines
  • Calibration can still be improved

Future work

  • Deeper Michigan architectures
  • Adaptive hidden-layer size

  • Larger benchmark suites
  • Neuron stability and interpretability analysis
